# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-23

Initial version of Bilbo.

- Local Markdown diary with editable tables, links, and tags.
- Global capture shortcut, capture archive, and captures embedded in notes.
- Search across notes and captures, including tags and capture insertion.
- Themes, configurable keyboard shortcuts, launch at login, and local diagnostic logs.
- Neco B logo and app icon.
